## About Me

I build practical tools for Kubernetes troubleshooting, everyday automation, and AI-assisted workflows. My focus is on making complex tasks easier to reproduce, inspect, and verify.

My projects range from local Kubernetes labs and spreadsheet reconciliation to customized agent tooling. Across these projects, I care about clear operational boundaries, useful failure feedback, and verifiable results.

## Engineering Focus

- **Reproducible troubleshooting:** local experiments, observable failures, and explicit recovery checks.
- **Controlled automation:** scoped permissions, approval gates, and audit trails for operational changes.
- **Reliable business workflows:** transaction boundaries, consistent inventory and payment records, and recoverable backups.

These themes connect my infrastructure projects, business applications, and agent tooling.
